Invoice AI

An invoice in one screen: who it is for, what you sold, the total — and a PDF to send. No account, no limits.

What it does

  • Invoices, estimates and receipts without limits, numbered automatically
  • Your details filled in once; clients suggested from past invoices
  • Bill to on several lines: name, address, the client's tax ID
  • Any currency, discount (% or amount), tax by its local name (VAT, GST, USt., TVA…), on top of prices or already included
  • Payment due: on receipt, 7 or 14 days, or your own date; a note on the invoice (payment terms, VAT exemption)
  • Estimate → invoice in one tap; mark paid → receipt
  • An invoice in 30 seconds with AI: describe the job in words, your AI app fills in the invoice
  • QR to pay on the invoice: SEPA, UPI, PIX, PromptPay or any payment link
  • Your signature and stamp on every invoice, estimate and receipt: sign with your finger, stamp from a photo
  • A report for your accountant: month, quarter or year as a PDF summary and a table
  • Branded invoice: your logo, all templates and your own color; document builder, notes for yourself, sending as ZIP
  • PDF without a watermark: share, print, save, send by email
  • All invoices: what you are still waiting for, overdue ones in red, a ready reminder with the PDF
  • Repeat an invoice in one tap; a cancelled invoice goes to Trash and can be restored, the number is never reused
  • Export all invoices as a table (CSV)
  • Invoice language separate from the app language, 22 languages, Arabic right to left

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays before the PDF of each new document, a report or a ZIP. Opening, sharing and printing a document you already made is free. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly (7 days free to try) or forever. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Your invoices stay on the phone

Invoices, clients, your details, signature and stamp are stored only inside the app and are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. A PDF leaves the phone only when you share, send or save it.
