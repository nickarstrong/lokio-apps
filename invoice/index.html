Invoice AI

An invoice in one screen: who it is for, what you sold, the total — and a PDF to send. No account, no limits.

Free, always

  • Invoices without limits, numbered automatically
  • Your details filled in once; clients suggested from past invoices
  • Bill to on several lines: name, address, the client's tax ID
  • Any currency, discount (% or amount), tax by its local name (VAT, GST, USt., TVA…), on top of prices or already included
  • Payment due: on receipt, 7 or 14 days, or your own date; a note on the invoice (payment terms, VAT exemption)
  • A full example you can show any time to see what goes where
  • PDF without a watermark: share, print, save, send by email
  • All invoices: what you are still waiting for, overdue ones in red, a ready reminder with the PDF
  • Repeat an invoice in one tap; a cancelled invoice goes to Trash and can be restored, the number is never reused
  • Export all invoices as a table (CSV)
  • Invoice language separate from the app language, 22 languages, Arabic right to left
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Estimates, turned into an invoice with one tap
  • Your logo in the PDF
  • Two more templates and your own color
  • Fill in with AI: describe the job in words, your AI app fills in the invoice
  • QR to pay on the invoice: SEPA, UPI, PIX, PromptPay or any payment link

Everything happens on the phone

Invoices, clients and your details are stored only inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything. A PDF leaves the phone only when you share, send or save it.
