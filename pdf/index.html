Compress PDF

A 174 MB book comes out at 4.3 MB — and the pages look the same.

What it does

  • Pictures inside the PDF get smaller; the text is left alone and stays sharp, selectable and searchable
  • Scans get a «Readable» mark: the smallest size at which small print is still easy to read
  • Editor metadata, duplicate data and hidden thumbnails are cleaned out
  • The result is shown before you press the button; files that are already compressed are marked
  • Copies go to Downloads / Compress PDF with «_compressed» in the name; originals are not touched
  • Compress and send in one tap; share all results at once
  • See every page large before you compress
  • Photos into one PDF under the limit: photos of a passport or a certificate become one PDF of exactly the size a form asks for
  • Many PDFs at once: one target for the whole stack, or each file to its own size
  • Scan in black & white: scanned pages become crisp black and white — several times lighter, small text stays sharp
  • Only the pages you need: tick pages on a grid with a magnifier, the rest stay out of the copy

Also: everything as one ZIP (even a single file), a password for the compressed copy, several PDFs joined into one under the limit, and a file that will not fit split into parts that do. Light and dark theme, 22 languages.

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each result. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Everything happens on the phone

PDFs are compressed on the phone itself; your documents are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
