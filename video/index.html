Compress Video

Smaller videos for chats and mail — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Pick a video or Share it here — each quality shows the size you’ll get before you start
  • High looks like the original, Normal is right for chats and mail, Small is 720p and the lightest
  • Never bigger than the original: if a video can’t get smaller without losing quality, the app says so
  • MP4 that plays everywhere; HDR videos from new phones become regular ones
  • Ready videos go to Movies / Compress Video; watch or share in one tap

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Fits WhatsApp, mail and Discord — 10, 16, 25, 50 MB or your own; still too big? «Make it 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • A frame for Reels, TikTok and Shorts — 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5
  • Only the part you need — start and end to a tenth of a second
  • Many videos at once, all in one archive
  • Also: without sound for an even smaller file

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The video is re-encoded by the phone’s own hardware. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. They leave the phone only when you share the file.
