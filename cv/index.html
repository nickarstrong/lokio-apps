Resume AI

Your own AI writes the resume, the app turns it into a clean PDF. No account.

What it does

  • Contacts with a photo, about, experience, education, skills, languages; drag to reorder, hide with one tap; your own sections
  • Skill levels from Junior to Expert and language levels from A1 to Native, shown as bars and written as words
  • Work format: open to relocation, remote, hybrid, on-site, business trips, work permit
  • A resume in 30 seconds with AI: one request to your AI app, paste the answer, every section is filled in; Improve with AI for About, each job and skills
  • Tailored to the job: paste a job posting, your AI rewrites About, experience and skills to match it, without inventing facts
  • Cover letter in your resume's colors, font and header, written by your AI for the job, saved as a PDF
  • Several resumes, one for each job: copy yours in one tap
  • All four templates, six colors, a serif font
  • A readiness score with a checklist of what is missing
  • The preview is the PDF: page borders are marked, one tap fits a slightly long resume on one page
  • <Name>_CV.pdf in Downloads / Resume; also Word (.docx) and the resume with the letter as one ZIP
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays before each saved file — PDF, Word or ZIP. Pro removes all ads.

How the AI part works

The app has no AI of its own. It prepares a request with a strict format and your current data. You tap Send, pick your AI app (ChatGPT, Gemini or any other), copy its answer and paste it back — the app puts every part in its place.

Your resume stays on the phone

Your resumes, job postings and letters are stored only inside the app and are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Text goes to an AI app only when you tap Send, and only to the app you choose.
