Resume AI

Your own AI writes the resume, the app turns it into a clean PDF. Nothing leaves the phone unless you send it.

Free, always

  • One resume by hand: contacts with a photo, about, experience, education, skills, languages
  • Drag sections to reorder, hide any of them with one tap
  • Skill levels from Junior to Expert and language levels from A1 to Native, shown as bars and written as words
  • Work format: open to relocation, remote, hybrid, on-site, business trips, work permit
  • The Strict template, made for job sites; the Column template free with your Lokio page
  • A readiness score with a checklist of what is missing
  • The preview is the PDF: page borders are marked, one tap fits a slightly long resume on one page
  • <Name>_CV.pdf in Downloads / Resume, no watermark; open, save anywhere, share or print
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • A resume in 30 seconds with AI: one request to your AI app, paste the answer, every section is filled in; Improve with AI for About, each job and skills
  • Tailored to the job: paste a job posting, your AI rewrites About, experience and skills to match it, without inventing facts
  • Cover letter in your resume's colors, font and header, written by your AI for the job, saved as a PDF
  • Several resumes, one for each job: copy yours in one tap
  • Resume in Word (.docx), when a job site or a recruiter asks for it
  • More: all four templates, six colors, a serif font; your own sections; the resume and the letter as one ZIP

How the AI part works

The app has no AI of its own and no internet. It prepares a request with a strict format and your current data. You tap Send, pick your AI app (ChatGPT, Gemini or any other), copy its answer and paste it back — the app puts every part in its place.

Everything happens on the phone

Your resumes, job postings and letters are stored only inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything. Text goes to an AI app only when you tap Send, and only to the app you choose.
