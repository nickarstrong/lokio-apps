PDF to Text

A 240-page book becomes 500 KB of clean text — ready for any AI chat or a note.

Free, always

  • One PDF at a time, any size — even a 200 MB book
  • Clean text: lines joined into paragraphs, words split at line ends restored, running headers and page numbers removed, spaced-out letters put back together
  • TXT for reading and notes, or Markdown with headings, lists and invisible page marks an AI can cite
  • Copy, share as a file, or save anywhere; results go to Downloads / PDF to Text
  • Get text and send in one tap; cancel a long job any time
  • See every page large before you start
  • Scans without a text layer are named honestly — no empty files
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Scan to text: pages that are only pictures are recognized right on the phone, offline — 12 scripts, from Latin and Cyrillic to Chinese, Arabic and Hindi
  • Many PDFs at once: a stack in one tap
  • Only the pages you need: tick pages on a grid with a magnifier, the text comes only from them
  • Parts for AI: a whole book in parts that fit an AI chat, each copied in one tap and marked [1/6], [2/6]…

Also in Pro: several PDFs as one text file, and all results in one ZIP.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so your PDFs physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
