PDF to Text

A 240-page book becomes 500 KB of clean text — ready for any AI chat or a note.

What it does

  • Any size — even a 200 MB book, read page by page
  • Clean text: lines joined into paragraphs, words split at line ends restored, running headers and page numbers removed, spaced-out letters put back together
  • TXT for reading and notes, or Markdown with headings, lists and invisible page marks an AI can cite
  • Scans: pages that are only pictures are recognized right on the phone — pick the script, from Latin and Cyrillic to Arabic, Greek, Hebrew and Thai
  • Many PDFs at once: text files or one ZIP
  • Only the pages you need, and parts that fit an AI chat, each copied in one tap
  • Copy, share as a file, or save anywhere; results go to Downloads / PDF to Text
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each result. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu.

Your files stay on the phone

PDFs are turned into text on the phone itself; your documents are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
