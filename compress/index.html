Compress Photo

Name the size in KB — and your photos come out at that size. Not «we tried».

Free, always

  • Exact size in KB or MB, and it actually lands — for the whole batch or for each photo
  • Was → now for every photo, before you tap and after
  • One button «Compress and send»: light copies go straight to the chat or the mail
  • No limit on the number of photos and no limit on file size
  • Any format: JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF; JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG
  • Pick by size and date: «from 5 MB», «older than a year»
  • Opens photos from Files, Telegram or mail with «Share» or «Open with»
  • The location is always removed from copies; date and camera — your choice
  • Originals are never changed or deleted after compressing
  • 22 languages, light and dark theme
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Document mode: the background is squeezed, the letters stay sharp
  • For social media 1:1 · 4:5 · 9:16: the whole photo in the frame, nothing cropped
  • Clean file names: photo_01, photo_02… with no date in the name
  • One archive: the whole batch as one ZIP, sent or saved to a card, USB drive or cloud folder
  • Also: WebP — the same photo, even lighter

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so your photos physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
