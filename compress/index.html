Compress Photo

Name the size in KB — and your photos come out at that size. Not «we tried».

Free, always

  • Exact size in KB or MB, and it actually lands
  • No limit on the number of photos and no limit on file size
  • Keeps the format: JPG stays JPG, PNG stays PNG, WebP stays WebP
  • Shot data kept or dropped — your choice
  • The whole batch as one archive, saved anywhere: card, USB drive, cloud folder
  • Light and dark theme, sixteen languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Gallery cleanup: pick photos older than a month or a year, replace them with light copies, see the space freed
  • Document mode: the background is squeezed, the letters stay readable

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so your photos physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
