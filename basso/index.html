Basso Player

Video & music player with home cinema sound

Bass you can feel — in your music, your videos and other music apps. No internet needed.

mdash; deep bass for your movies, clips and music. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Plays the music and videos already on your phone: songs, artists, folders, recent
  • Two knobs: Bass (how much) and Depth (how low) — deep bass without distortion
  • Bass on videos too: clips and movies sound like a cinema
  • A big live equalizer that moves with the track
  • Modes: Bass, Voice, Hall, Cinema, Concert, Compressed track
  • Two looks: Glitch and Vinyl · 22 languages

Pro

  • Bass in other music apps — one switch, for players that allow sound effects; «All phone sound» where the phone allows it
  • DJ deck: mixer, KILL for low, mid and high, filter, a disc you can scratch, tempo with keylock
  • Four more looks: Cinema, Speaker, 3D, Retro
  • Month, year or forever — your choice. No trial, no ads.

Optional: leave a tip of any size. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your music or anything else. Bass in other apps changes only how their sound plays on your phone; Basso does not record or read it.
