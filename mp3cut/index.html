MP3 Cutter

Cut a song or a voice note — and make it your ringtone.

What it does

  • See the whole track as a wave; drag two handles, fine-tune with ±0.1 s
  • Listen to the part before saving
  • Save it as MP3 — songs, voice notes and the sound of videos
  • Set it as your ringtone, notification or alarm in one tap
  • A soft ringtone — cut from mid-song? «Make it soft» right on the Done screen
  • Cut out the middle — remove a pause or a mistake and keep both ends
  • Louder — 150% or 200% for quiet tracks, peaks softened
  • Quality 128, 192 or 320 kbps. Ready files go to Music / MP3 Cutter; next in one tap: join tracks, speech to text

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each saved file. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

Your files are processed on the phone and are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. The app asks for «Modify system settings» only when you set a ringtone.
