MP3 Cutter

Cut a song or a voice note — and make it your ringtone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • See the whole track as a wave; drag two handles, fine-tune with ±0.1 s
  • Listen to the part before saving
  • Save it as MP3 — songs and voice notes
  • Set it as your ringtone, notification or alarm in one tap
  • Ready files go to Music / MP3 Cutter; next in one tap: join tracks, speech to text

Pro — one-time purchase

  • A soft ringtone — cut from mid-song? «Make it soft» right on the Done screen
  • Cut out the middle — remove an ad, a pause or a mistake
  • Louder — 150% or 200% for quiet tracks, peaks softened
  • Sound from a video — a piece of a clip or a Reels as a ringtone
  • Also: quality 128 or 320 kbps

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files. It asks for «Modify system settings» only when you set a ringtone.
