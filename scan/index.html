Scan to PDF

Point the camera at a sheet — get a clean, straight PDF.

What it does

  • A live frame shows the sheet the app has found; you press the button when you are ready — no automatic shots
  • The main camera, tap to focus, flash
  • Three looks: Document (clean black and white), Color (white paper, stamps and pen stay in colour), As shot
  • Crop by the corners with a magnifier, rotate, retake, delete, reorder pages by holding a thumbnail
  • Photos and PDFs you already have: a photo from a chat is cleaned like a scan, pages of a digital PDF are kept whole — many at once
  • PDF under the size limit: 1, 2 or 5 MB for websites and mail — the pages get lighter until the whole PDF fits
  • ID and passport: front and back on one sheet at real size, like a photocopy
  • Any sheet size: A4, Letter or no margins; 3200 px pages for print
  • Scan_<date>_<time>.pdf in Downloads / Scan to PDF; open, save anywhere, share, print or send as one ZIP
  • No watermark, light and dark theme, 22 languages

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each PDF. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu.

Best results

A sheet on a darker background, in even light.

Your pages stay on the phone

The app asks for the camera only when you tap Scan. Pages are made on the phone itself and are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
