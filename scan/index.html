Scan to PDF

Point the camera at a sheet — get a clean, straight PDF. Nothing leaves the phone.

Free, always

  • A live frame shows the sheet the app has found; you press the button when you are ready — no automatic shots
  • The main camera, tap to focus, flash
  • Three looks: Document (clean black and white), Color (white paper, stamps and pen stay in colour), As shot
  • Crop by the corners with a magnifier, rotate, retake, delete, reorder pages by holding a thumbnail
  • Zoom a page with two fingers or a double tap; hold it to see the original
  • Photos and PDFs you already have: a photo from a chat is cleaned like a scan, pages of a digital PDF are kept whole
  • Scan_<date>_<time>.pdf in Downloads / Scan to PDF; open, save anywhere, share or print
  • Any number of scans and pages, no watermark, light and dark theme, sixteen languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Many photos and PDFs at once
  • Any sheet size: A4, Letter or no margins (the page is exactly the size of the document)
  • 3200 px pages for print

Best results

A sheet on a darker background, in even light.

Everything happens on the phone

The app asks for the camera only when you tap Scan. It has no internet permission: your pages physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
