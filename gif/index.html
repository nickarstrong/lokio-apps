Video to GIF

Turn any moment of a video into a GIF — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Choose the moment with two handles on the frames, exact to 0.1 s — up to 10 seconds
  • Size 320, 480 or 640 px and 10, 15 or 20 frames per second; the button shows about how big the GIF will be
  • The GIF plays right away — watch it before sending
  • No watermark. Ready GIFs go to Pictures / Video to GIF

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Longer — up to 30 seconds in one GIF
  • Fit a size — under 2, 5, 10 or 15 MB, the GIF shrinks itself to fit a chat’s limit
  • Boomerang — forward and back, again and again
  • Caption — your text on top or at the bottom, white with a black outline

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. The phone’s own decoder reads the video, and the app writes the GIF itself.
