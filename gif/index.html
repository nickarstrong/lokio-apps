Video to GIF

Turn any moment of a video into a GIF — right on your phone. Every feature free.

What it does

  • Choose the moment with two handles on the frames, exact to 0.1 s — up to 30 seconds
  • Size 320, 480 or 640 px and 10, 15 or 20 frames per second; the button shows about how big the GIF will be
  • The GIF plays right away — watch it before sending
  • Or an MP4 loop — the moment 1, 3 or 5 times in a row, lighter and sharper, for Stories and statuses
  • No watermark. Ready GIFs go to Pictures / Video to GIF
  • Fits Discord and chats — under 2, 5, 10 or 15 MB; too big? «Make it 10 MB» right on the Done screen
  • Lead the frame — vertical, square, a circle (transparent GIF sticker) or your own; hold it and lead it with your finger while the video plays, and the GIF follows the action
  • Stickers — your logo, a GIF, a drawing or text on top, as many as you like; move, resize, rotate; lower the opacity for a watermark
  • Boomerang — forward and back, again and again

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: one short ad plays for each GIF or MP4 loop. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

Your videos are never uploaded; the internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. The phone’s own decoder reads the video, and the app writes the GIF itself.
