Email Maker

A branded email in your color, with a signature and a button, made on the phone and sent from your own Gmail or any mail app.

Free, always

  • Your brand once: company, name, color, logo letters, role, phone, email, site, messengers
  • The Letter template; the Letterhead template free with your Lokio page
  • An HTML signature for any email you write in Gmail
  • Mailing list in Bcc: paste addresses, load a list from Excel or CSV, or open the table with Email Maker from your files; duplicates removed
  • A counter against the 500-a-day Gmail limit and tips before sending: missing subject, empty links, CAPS, big attachments
  • Attachments: PDFs and photos, or files shared from any app
  • Save any email as your own template
  • Light and dark preview, 22 languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission, no «sent from» line

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Email with AI in a minute: one request to your AI app, paste the answer — subject, text, price and button are filled in
  • Offer with a price and announcement: the Offer and Announcement templates and the email builder (add, remove and drag blocks)
  • Attachments under the mail limit: heavy photos and PDFs shrink by themselves so the email fits into 25 MB
  • Several brands and saved mailing groups
  • More: all colors, your own color from a palette, HEX or an image, a serif font, attachments as one archive

How sending works

The app puts the finished email on the clipboard and opens your mail app with the recipients, subject and attachments already in place. You long-press the message field, tap Paste and send. The email goes from your own account; the app never sends anything itself.

Everything happens on the phone

Your brand, emails and lists are stored only inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything.
