Video to MP3

The sound from any video as MP3 — right on your phone. Every feature free.

What it does

  • Pick a video or Share it here — the size shows before you start
  • MP3 at 192 kbps plays everywhere: phones, cars, computers — no limits
  • A video without sound? The app says so right away, saves nothing and shows no ad
  • Ready files go to Music / Video to MP3; listen or share in one tap
  • Next in one tap: cut a ringtone, join tracks, turn speech into text
  • Only the part you need — just the chorus or the speech, to a tenth of a second
  • Fits WhatsApp and mail — 16 or 25 MB; too big? «Make it 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • Louder — 150% or 200% for quiet recordings, peaks softened
  • Many at once, all in one archive
  • Voice notes and audio files → MP3, M4A without re-encoding, 128 or 320 kbps

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each saved file. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The sound is decoded and encoded on the phone itself; your files are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
