Video to MP3

The sound from any video as MP3 — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Pick a video or an audio file, or Share it here — the size shows before you start
  • MP3 at 192 kbps plays everywhere: phones, cars, computers
  • M4A keeps the sound exactly as in the original and is ready in a moment
  • Voice notes, WAV, OGG and M4A files become MP3 too
  • A video without sound? The app says so right away and saves nothing
  • Ready files go to Music / Video to MP3; listen or share in one tap

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Many files at once, one setting for all, and one ZIP for chats
  • Only a part — just the song or the speech, start and end to a tenth of a second
  • Quality 128 or 320 kbps
  • Louder — 150% or 200% for quiet recordings, peaks softened

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The sound is decoded and encoded on the phone itself. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files. They leave the phone only when you share them.
