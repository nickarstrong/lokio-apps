Unlock PDF

Remove a PDF password you know, set or change one — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Remove a password you know: type it once, get a copy that opens without it
  • Print and copy restrictions are removed too — no password needed for those
  • Set a password so only people who know it can open the PDF
  • Change a password: the old one off, a new one on, in one step
  • Files go to Downloads / Unlock PDF; your original stays as it was
  • Next in one tap: merge, compress or sign the unlocked PDF

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Many PDFs at once: one password for a whole stack of statements
  • My passwords: remember a password, encrypted on the phone, and new files from the same bank open by themselves
  • Remove hidden data: author, program and dates are wiped from the file
  • No printing and no copying for PDFs you lock

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

It does not guess passwords

The app works only with a password you already know. If you don't know it, ask whoever sent the file.

Everything happens on the phone

Files are unlocked and locked on the phone itself. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your documents or passwords. They leave the phone only when you share them.
