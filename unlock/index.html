Unlock PDF

Remove a PDF password you know, set or change one — right on your phone.

What it does

  • Remove a password you know: type it once, get a copy that opens without it
  • Print and copy restrictions are removed too — no password needed for those
  • Set a password so only people who know it can open the PDF; block printing or copying if you like
  • Change a password: the old one off, a new one on, in one step
  • Many PDFs at once: one password for a whole stack of statements
  • My passwords: remember a password, encrypted on the phone, and new files from the same bank open by themselves; typed the same password again? «Remember this password» is right on the Done screen
  • Remove hidden data: author, program and dates are wiped from the file
  • Files go to Downloads / Unlock PDF; your original stays as it was. Next in one tap: turn the unlocked PDF into Word or pictures, merge it or make it smaller

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each saved PDF. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

It does not guess passwords

The app works only with a password you already know. If you don't know it, ask whoever sent the file.

Everything happens on the phone

Files are unlocked and locked on the phone itself; your documents and passwords are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
