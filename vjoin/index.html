Merge Video

Join videos and photos into one MP4 — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Merge any number of videos
  • Vertical and horizontal, 4K and videos from chats go into one frame — nothing is stretched
  • Drag to set the order, watch the whole merge before saving
  • Same quality as your best video, made by the phone’s own encoder
  • No watermark. Ready videos go to Movies / Merge Video

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Photos and videos in one clip — photos between videos, each 2–8 s on screen
  • Stickers — your logo, a GIF, a drawing or text on the whole video, with opacity for a watermark
  • Frame for Reels, TikTok and Shorts — 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9, and fill the frame without bars
  • Trim each video — start and end to a tenth of a second
  • Fit a size — 8, 10, 25, 50 MB or your own, for mail and messengers

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. The phone’s own video encoder does the work.
