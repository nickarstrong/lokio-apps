PDF to Word

PDF to an editable .docx — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Pick a PDF or Share it here — you get a .docx that opens in Word, Google Docs or WPS
  • Headings stay headings, lists stay lists, tables become Word tables, images stay in place
  • Page numbers and repeating headers are removed, broken lines are joined back into paragraphs
  • The file goes to Downloads / PDF to Word; open or share it in one tap
  • A scan with no text inside? The app tells you so honestly instead of making an empty file

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Many PDFs at once, each one its own Word file
  • Only the pages you need — for example 5–12 out of 300
  • PDFs with a password — the password is used once and never stored

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The PDF becomes a Word file on the phone itself. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your documents. They leave the phone only when you share the file.
