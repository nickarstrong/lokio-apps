PDF to Word

PDF to an editable .docx — right on your phone.

What it does

  • Pick a PDF or Share it here — you get a .docx that opens in Word, Google Docs or WPS
  • Headings stay headings, lists stay lists, tables become Word tables, images stay in place
  • Page numbers and repeating headers are removed, broken lines are joined back into paragraphs
  • Scans to Word — a photo of a contract or a scanned page is recognized right on the phone
  • PDFs with a password — the password is used once and never stored
  • Only the pages you need — for example 5–12 out of 300
  • Many PDFs at once, each one its own Word file, or the whole stack as one ZIP
  • The file goes to Downloads / PDF to Word; open or share it in one tap
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each result. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu.

Your files stay on the phone

The PDF becomes a Word file on the phone itself; your documents are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
