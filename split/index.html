Video Splitter

Split a video into parts for statuses and Stories — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Parts of 30, 60 or 90 s or 2 to 10 equal parts — the strip of frames shows every cut and the number of each part
  • Instant: parts are copied without re-encoding, so quality and file size stay exactly like the original
  • Watch any part before saving
  • Each part is a separate video with its number in the name, so you post them in order
  • Share all at once or one by one; no watermark. Ready parts go to Movies / Video Splitter

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Fits WhatsApp and mail — every part under 16 or 25 MB; parts too big? «Make each 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • By scenes — drag handles to the exact frame and keep only the parts you need
  • Frame 9:16 — horizontal video into vertical parts, the frame moves with your finger
  • All in one ZIP — for chats that take only one file
  • Also: 10 MB for Discord, 50 MB for forms

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. Parts are copied as they are; only size, scenes and 9:16 use the phone’s own video encoder.
