Video Splitter

Split a video into parts for statuses and Stories — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Parts of 15, 30 or 60 s — the strip of frames shows every cut and the number of each part
  • Watch any part before saving
  • Each part is a separate video with its number in the name, so you post them in order
  • Share all at once or one by one; no watermark, quality stays like the original
  • Ready parts go to Movies / Video Splitter

Pro — one-time purchase

  • By size — every part under 8, 16, 25 or 50 MB
  • Equal parts — 2 to 10 parts of the same length
  • Your own cuts — tap the strip exactly where the scene changes
  • Frame 9:16 — horizontal video into vertical parts, the frame moves with your finger
  • All in one ZIP — for chats that take only one file

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. It uses the phone’s own video encoder — fast and cool.
