Stamp & Sign

Photograph your stamp on paper once — then put it on any PDF with one tap.

Everything it does

  • A clean stamp from a photo: the app finds the ink, removes the paper, shadows and background, and keeps the colour — no sliders
  • Works with the camera, the gallery and screenshots
  • Draw a signature with your finger: three widths, three colours
  • Tap a stamp and it lands on the page; drag it, resize it, tilt it like a real one; zoom the page with two fingers
  • A new file <name>_signed.pdf in Downloads / Stamp & Sign; the text of the document stays text, the original is not changed
  • Any number of stamps, signatures and documents, no watermark
  • Share and save the signed PDF
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages
  • No account
  • Photo of a document → sign: photos of paper pages become one PDF right in the app, ready for your stamp and signature
  • Date and name: today’s date, your full name or any line next to the signature, placed like a stamp
  • Every page or the last one in one tap
  • Many PDFs at once: the same stamp in the same place on every file
  • Send the result as one ZIP

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each signed document, an archive is one more. Pro removes all ads — 7 days free on the yearly plan, or by the month or forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Best results

A stamp on white paper, photographed from above in daylight.

Not a digital certificate

The app places an image of your stamp and signature on the page, like on paper. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature.

Everything happens on the phone

Documents, stamps and signatures stay on the phone: the app uploads nothing, and it has no camera permission — photos are taken by your phone’s camera app. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases.
