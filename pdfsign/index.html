Stamp & Sign

Photograph your stamp on paper once — then put it on any PDF with one tap.

Free, always

  • A clean stamp from a photo: the app finds the ink, removes the paper, shadows and background, and keeps the colour — no sliders
  • Works with the camera, the gallery and screenshots
  • Draw a signature with your finger: three widths, three colours
  • Tap a stamp and it lands on the page; drag it, resize it, tilt it like a real one; zoom the page with two fingers
  • A new file <name>_signed.pdf in Downloads / Stamp & Sign; the text of the document stays text, the original is not changed
  • One stamp and one signature, any number of documents, no watermark
  • Light and dark theme, sixteen languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Unlimited stamps and signatures
  • All pages or the last page in one tap
  • Many PDFs at once: the same stamp in the same place on every file

Best results

A stamp on white paper, photographed from above in daylight.

Not a digital certificate

The app places an image of your stamp and signature on the page, like on paper. It is not a certificate-based electronic signature.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission and no camera permission: photos are taken by your phone’s camera app. Your documents, stamps and signatures physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
