Photo to Text

Text from any photo — right on your phone. A page, a sign, a slide, a receipt or a screenshot. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Take a photo, pick one from the gallery, or Share a screenshot from any app — the text is ready at once
  • Run your finger over the lines you need right on the photo — copy, share or listen to just them
  • Phone numbers, email, links and addresses light up: tap to call, write, open the site or the map
  • Any language: Latin, Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Devanagari, Arabic, Greek, Hebrew, Thai, Armenian, Georgian
  • Flowing text: lines joined into paragraphs, split words fixed — paste into a chat, a document or an AI as is
  • Edit the text, copy, share, save as TXT; Listen reads it aloud in Read Aloud
  • Open a photo with Photo to Text straight from the Gallery or Files
  • Recent texts with search; one photo at a time with no limit; no ads, no account

Pro — one-time purchase, no subscription

  • Many photos at once: up to 50 in a row become one text, in order — a lecture, a contract, a book chapter
  • To Word: the text as a .docx file with paragraphs, ready to edit in Word or Google Docs
  • Business card → contact: name, phones, email and company into Contacts in one tap
  • And more: a batch as one ZIP with a TXT for every photo

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps without ads.

Everything happens on the phone

Text is recognized on the phone itself. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your photos or text. They leave the phone only when you share them.
