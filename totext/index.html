To Text

Speech becomes text — right on your phone. Record a lecture, a meeting or any video, and the text appears while you record. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Sound around you: lectures, meetings, conversations, outdoors — tap and recording starts
  • Sound on your phone: videos, voice messages, podcasts, webinars — no links, no downloads
  • Text appears while you record; pause the parts you do not need
  • Audio and video files, or Share from Telegram, WhatsApp or the gallery
  • Copy, send to any AI for a summary, save as TXT or MD; tap a phrase to hear it
  • Speech language detected by itself; no minute limits
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Floating button over any app: ▶ record, tap to pause, ■ stop — 3 full trial recordings free
  • Translation to English, on the phone
  • Everything new in Pro, included

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps without ads.

Everything happens on the phone

Speech recognition runs on the phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your recordings or text. A recording leaves the phone only when you share it.
