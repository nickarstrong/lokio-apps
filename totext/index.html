To Text

Speech becomes text — right on your phone. Record a lecture, a meeting or any video, and the text appears while you record. Speech recognition runs on the phone.

What it does

  • Sound around you: lectures, meetings, conversations, outdoors — tap and recording starts
  • Sound on your phone: videos, voice messages, podcasts, webinars — no links, no downloads
  • Text appears while you record; pause the parts you do not need
  • Audio and video files, or Share from Telegram, WhatsApp or the gallery
  • Copy, send to any AI for a summary, save as TXT, MD, SRT subtitles or Word; tap a phrase to hear it; Listen sends the text to Read Aloud
  • Open a recording with To Text straight from your files
  • Speech in 22 languages, detected by itself: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, Dutch, Romanian, Czech, Arabic, Urdu, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai
  • Recording and text without limits: long lectures, meetings, webinars and podcasts get the whole text; no account
  • Floating button over any app: ▶ record, tap to pause, ■ stop
  • Translation to English, on the phone
  • SRT subtitles for your own video: open the video in To Text and «Save SRT» right on the text screen — a subtitle file with time codes for CapCut, YouTube, Premiere
  • To Word: the text as a .docx file with paragraphs

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays before each new text — a recording, a file or a translation. Copying, saving and sharing a text is free. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly (7 days free to try) or forever. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu.

Everything happens on the phone

Speech recognition runs on the phone; your recordings and text are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. A recording leaves the phone only when you share it.
