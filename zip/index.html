Zip Extractor

Open ZIP, RAR and 7Z from Telegram, WhatsApp or mail — right on your phone.

Free

  • Open ZIP, RAR (including RAR5), 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ISO and CAB
  • Photos and videos inside show as tiles, other files as a list with their folders
  • Tap any file to open it — no unpacking first
  • Save everything to Downloads / Zip Extractor in one tap, folders as in the archive
  • Password archives — open ZIP, RAR and 7Z with a password
  • Pack into ZIP or 7Z — photos and files in one archive, with a password if you like
  • Fits in an email — shrink photos, videos and PDFs to 25, 10 or 5 MB, or split into several archives
  • Only the files you need — tick them, save or send just those
  • Photos straight to the gallery — iPhone HEIC photos become JPG
  • No account

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each result — saving, packing, sending to the gallery. Pro removes the ads: monthly, yearly or once forever.

Everything happens on the phone

Your archives and files are opened and packed on the phone and never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Archive passwords are never stored.
