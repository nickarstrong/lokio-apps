Zip Extractor

Open ZIP, RAR and 7Z from Telegram, WhatsApp or mail — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Open ZIP, RAR (including RAR5), 7Z, TAR, GZ, BZ2, XZ, ISO and CAB
  • Photos and videos inside show as tiles, other files as a list with their folders
  • Tap any file to open it — no unpacking first
  • Save everything to Downloads / Zip Extractor in one tap, folders as in the archive
  • No ads, no account

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Password archives — open ZIP, RAR and 7Z with a password
  • Pack into ZIP — photos and files in one archive, with a password if you like
  • Only the files you need — tick them, save or send just those
  • Photos straight to the gallery — iPhone HEIC photos become JPG

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files. Archive passwords are never stored.
