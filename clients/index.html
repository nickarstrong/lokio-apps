Clients

Every client and every deadline on one screen — what is due comes first.

Free, always

  • Up to 3 clients, one card per client: avatar or logo, tax ID, contracts with number, sum and currency, the term “from — to” or open-ended
  • Cards built from blocks: text, number, date, deadline, sum, percent, checklist, payment by month, contact — you name things your way, in any country
  • Deadlines remind you N days before and repeat monthly, quarterly or yearly; one notification a day, only when something is due
  • Remind a client in one tap by WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS or e-mail — the text says what is due and what is missing
  • A comment for yourself on every client
  • Quick actions: Invoice, Sign and Letter in our apps with the client details copied; deadlines to your calendar (.ics)
  • Backup to a file, restore, export to Excel (CSV)
  • Light and dark theme, 22 languages
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission at all

Pro

Pro by the year (7 days free to try), by the month or once forever:

  • Unlimited clients
  • Documents from clients: a photo or PDF shared from WhatsApp, Telegram or mail goes into the client card and ticks the item it closes; documents by month, ZIP of the month
  • Money: contracts per month by currency and who has not paid you
  • Tax, My fee and Liquidation cards
  • Import clients from Excel (CSV)

Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Everything stays on the phone

The app has no internet permission. Your clients, contracts and documents physically cannot be uploaded anywhere. Check it yourself on the permissions page in Android settings.
