EXIF Cleaner

See what your photos give away, and send them clean. Nothing leaves the phone.

Every photo carries hidden data: where it was taken, when, on which phone, often the camera's serial number. Anyone you send it to can read it. EXIF Cleaner shows which of your photos reveal a location and makes clean copies — on the phone, with no account.

What it does

Marks the photos that give away where they were taken. Shows exactly what is written in each one. Removes location, serial numbers, date, camera and author — everything by default, or only what you tick. Cleans a whole batch at once, and can replace the originals so the Gallery has no duplicates. JPG, PNG and WebP are cleaned without re-compression.

What it does not do

The app has no internet permission at all. No server, no analytics, no account. Your photos physically cannot leave the phone.
