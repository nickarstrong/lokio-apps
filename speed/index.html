Video Speed

Slow down or speed up a video — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • One speed for the whole video: 0.25×, 0.5×, 0.75×, 1.5×, 2× or 4×, or anything in between with the slider
  • The voice keeps its pitch — the tempo changes, not the voice
  • The player plays it exactly as it will be saved; the new length is shown before you save
  • Same quality as the original, no watermark. Ready videos go to Movies / Video Speed

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Moment — slow down or speed up just one part, with smooth transitions: slow-mo, velocity, speed-up
  • 0.1× to 10× — super slow-mo and timelapse
  • Fit into 15, 30, 60 or 90 seconds — the speed is picked for a status or Stories
  • Stickers — your logo, a GIF, a drawing or text on top; lower the opacity for a watermark
  • Reverse — the video plays backwards (finished video up to 60 s, no sound)

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. The phone’s own encoder does the work.
