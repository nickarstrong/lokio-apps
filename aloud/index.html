Read Aloud

Any text, read aloud — right on your phone. Books, PDFs, articles and notes, with the phrase being read highlighted.

What it does

  • EPUB, FB2, PDF and TXT books and documents; paste any text
  • Share an article from the browser, mail or a messenger — it is read at once
  • The phrase being read is highlighted; tap any phrase to read from there
  • Keeps reading with the screen off and in other apps; headphone buttons work
  • Sleep timer: 15 or 30 minutes, end of chapter, or your own time up to 10 hours
  • Remembers your place in every book; speed from 0.5× to 3×
  • Every voice of your phone; no minute limits, no account
  • Save audio — the whole text or a chapter as an audio file in Music: for the car, the player or to send; at the end of a text «Save audio» is right there
  • Every evening, on its own — a reading schedule: the book starts where you stopped and turns off on time
  • Listen over any app — a floating button over YouTube, a feed or a browser: play, pause, sleep timer
  • Names and abbreviations said right — a pronunciation dictionary; home screen widget

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays before each new text and each audio file; going on with a text is free. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly (7 days free to try) or forever. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu.

Everything happens on the phone

The text is read by the speech engine of your phone. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. A file leaves the phone only when you share it.
