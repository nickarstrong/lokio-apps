Read Aloud

Any text, read aloud — right on your phone. Books, PDFs, articles and notes, with the phrase being read highlighted. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • EPUB, FB2, PDF and TXT books and documents; paste any text
  • Share an article from the browser, mail or a messenger — it is read at once
  • The phrase being read is highlighted; tap any phrase to read from there
  • Keeps reading with the screen off and in other apps; headphone buttons work
  • Sleep timer: 15 or 30 minutes, end of chapter, or your own time up to 10 hours
  • Remembers your place in every book; speed from 0.5× to 3×
  • Every voice of your phone; no minute limits, no ads, no account

Pro — 7 days free on the yearly plan, or by the month or forever

  • Listen in the car and send it — the whole text or a chapter as an audio file in Music; at the end of a text «Save audio» is right there
  • Every evening, on its own — a reading schedule: the book starts where you stopped and turns off on time; one tap when the sleep timer goes off
  • Listen over any app — a floating button over YouTube, a feed or a browser: play, pause, sleep timer
  • Names and abbreviations said right — a pronunciation dictionary
  • Also: home screen widget

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps without ads.

Everything happens on the phone

The text is read by the speech engine of your phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files or text. A file leaves the phone only when you share it.
