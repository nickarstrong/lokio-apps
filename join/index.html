Merge Audio

Join songs and voice notes into one MP3 — right on your phone.

Free

  • Join any number of songs, voice notes and the sound of videos
  • MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG — different formats and quality go into one file
  • Back to back, no pause between files — or a pause of 0.5 to 5 seconds for lessons and audiobooks
  • Crossfade — each song fades into the next, 1 to 10 seconds
  • Same loudness — quiet voice notes and loud songs sound equally loud
  • Fits WhatsApp and mail — 16 or 25 MB; too big? «Make it 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • Quality 128, 192 or 320 kbps
  • Drag to set the order, hear every join before saving
  • Ready files go to Music / Merge Audio; next in one tap: ringtone, speech to text
  • No account

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each merged file. Pro removes the ads: monthly, yearly or once forever.

Everything happens on the phone

Your audio is joined on the phone and never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. The phone’s own decoder and the open-source LAME encoder do the work.
