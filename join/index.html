Merge Audio

Join songs and voice notes into one MP3 — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Join any number of songs and voice notes
  • MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG — different formats and quality go into one file
  • Back to back, no pause between files
  • Drag to set the order, hear every join before saving
  • Ready files go to Music / Merge Audio; next in one tap: ringtone, speech to text

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Crossfade — each song fades into the next, 1 to 10 seconds
  • Same loudness — quiet voice notes and loud songs sound equally loud
  • Fits WhatsApp and mail — 16 or 25 MB; too big? «Make it 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • Pause between files — 0.5 to 5 seconds for lessons and audiobooks
  • Also: the sound of videos, quality 128 or 320 kbps

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your files. The phone’s own decoder and the open-source LAME encoder do the work.
