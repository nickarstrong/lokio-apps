Isoba: Dollar & Crisis Index

One honest bearing instead of a chaos of opinions: what is really happening with the dollar, credit and the economy.

Free, forever

  • Dollar index (DSI) 0–100 — a coordinate of the dollar system, not an exchange-rate forecast
  • Crisis meter — stress already present in credit, banks, jobs and the economy
  • What’s ahead — how often a 20% market fall or a US recession followed the same zone in the past
  • What changed this week — in plain words
  • “Why” on every number: what it is made of and its history

Pro

Eight filters, Fed series, history by day since 1990 and a breakdown of any day — 12 more cards for your dashboard. Subscription with cancel anytime in Google Play; the first 30 days everything is open, no card needed.

Open calculation

Every day after the US close the result is saved as a public, dated record in git: history of isoba/data.json. It cannot be changed after the fact. Not investment advice.
