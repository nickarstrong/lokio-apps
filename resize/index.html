Resizer

Any photo into any format — exact pixels. The photo sits in the frame, you move it with a finger, Save and send gives exactly what you see.

Free, always

  • Square 1:1, portrait 4:5, story 9:16, wide 16:9, cover 1200×630, A4 at 300 dpi
  • Your own size: drag the corners of the frame, then type exact pixels — or centimeters at any dpi
  • Drag to move, pinch to zoom in or out, double tap to fill the frame again, rotate by 90°
  • Fit with borders: the whole photo, nothing cut off — blurred, white or black borders
  • Transparent PNG, WebP and GIF stay transparent — or go on white or black
  • JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP in; JPEG or PNG out, chosen for you
  • Date and camera kept or removed in one switch; location is never copied
  • Size and format of every photo on its thumbnail
  • Save and send in one tap; open a photo with Resizer straight from the Gallery or Files
  • 22 languages, Arabic and Urdu right to left
  • No ads, no account, no internet permission

Pro — one-time purchase, no subscription

  • Batch for the feed and marketplace: the whole album in one format in one tap
  • Faces always in the frame: found on the phone, no internet, across the whole batch
  • Panorama into a carousel: 2 to 10 seamless slides, 4:5 or 1:1, in swipe order
  • Ready to send: the file fits the size a site or form asks for — from 200 KB to 5 MB
  • And more: every format from one photo at once, everything as one archive

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps without ads.

Everything happens on the phone

Photos are opened through the Android photo picker and never leave the phone. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload anything. New files go to Pictures / Resizer; the originals are not touched.
