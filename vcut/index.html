Video Cutter

Trim a video and crop it for Reels — right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • See the video as a strip of frames; drag two handles, fine-tune with ±0.1 s
  • The player shows the exact frame under the handle; the magnifier zooms in for a frame-exact cut
  • Keep the part — or cut out the middle, the ends are joined
  • Frame 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9 — move the frame with your finger so faces stay in
  • No watermark; quality stays like the original; ready videos go to Movies / Video Cutter

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Fits WhatsApp and mail — 16 or 25 MB; still too big? «Make it 16 MB» right on the Done screen
  • Fit whole — the whole video in 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5 with blurred edges instead of black bars
  • Rotate and mirror — a sideways video upright in one tap
  • Frame to photo — any moment as a full-quality picture, with your crop and rotation
  • Also: without sound

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your videos. It uses the phone’s own video encoder — fast and cool.
