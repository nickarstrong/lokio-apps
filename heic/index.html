HEIC to JPG Batch Converter

Convert iPhone photos on your Android phone. Nothing is uploaded.

HEIC is Apple's photo format. Android shows it, but upload forms, printers and Windows often refuse it. This app turns a whole batch into ordinary JPG — on the phone, with no account and no internet upload.

What it does

Open it and every HEIC photo on the phone is already there as a grid, newest first, filtered by folder. Tap the ones you want. Convert them at the quality you choose. Send the whole batch in one go, or save it as a single ZIP. Date and camera info are kept unless you turn that off.

What it does not do

It reads images so it can show them to you, and nothing else: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts. It has no server, no analytics and no ads, and your photos never leave the phone.
