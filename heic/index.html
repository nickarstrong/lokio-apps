HEIC to JPG Batch Converter

Convert iPhone photos on your Android phone. Nothing is uploaded.

HEIC is Apple's photo format. Android shows it, but upload forms, printers and Windows often refuse it. This app turns a whole batch into ordinary JPG — on the phone, with no account and no internet upload.

What it does

Pick any number of HEIC photos, or scan a whole folder. Convert them at the quality you choose. Send the whole batch in one go, or save it as a single ZIP. Date and camera info are kept unless you turn that off.

What it does not do

It has no access to your gallery, your storage or your camera. It reads only the files you hand it. It has no server, no analytics and no ads.
