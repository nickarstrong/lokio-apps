HEIC Converter

iPhone photos to JPG, PNG and WebP on your Android phone. Nothing is uploaded.

HEIC is Apple's photo format. Android shows it, but upload forms, printers and Windows often refuse it. This app turns a whole batch into ordinary JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF — on the phone, with no account and no internet.

What it does

Open it and every photo on the phone is there as a grid, with its format and size on each tile, so you see at once which ones are HEIC. Tick the ones you want and tap “Convert & send”: one button converts them and opens Share. Copies stay in Pictures › HEIC Converter. Date and camera info are kept unless you turn that off; location is never copied.

Pro — one-time purchase

JPG under a size limit (1, 2 or 5 MB, for forms and mail) · PDF from photos · frames for social media (1:1, 4:5, 9:16, the whole photo, no cropping) · the whole batch as one ZIP. Also: JPG and PNG back to HEIC (half the size), “Max” quality and file names by date taken.

What it does not do

It reads images so it can show them to you, and nothing else: no camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts. It has no internet permission, no server, no analytics and no ads, and your photos never leave the phone.
