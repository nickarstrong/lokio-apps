Merge PDF

Merge and split PDFs — free, right on your phone. No internet needed.

Free, always

  • Merge several PDFs and photos into one PDF — every photo becomes a page
  • Drag to set the order, remove a file, add more; Share files here from any app
  • Split a PDF three ways: every page as its own file, chosen pages as a new PDF, or ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10
  • Files go to Downloads / Merge PDF with clear names; share, open or print them
  • Next in one tap: make the file smaller, add a stamp or signature, pull out the text
  • No limits on files or pages, no watermark, no account

Pro — one-time purchase

  • Passwords: open password-protected PDFs and lock your own
  • Page tools: rotate, remove and reorder pages in one PDF
  • Page numbers and a watermark across every page

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

Files are merged and split on the phone itself. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot upload your documents. They leave the phone only when you share them.
