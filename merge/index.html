Merge PDF

Merge and split PDFs — right on your phone. Every feature free.

What it does

  • Merge several PDFs and photos into one PDF — every photo becomes a page
  • Drag to set the order, remove a file, add more; Share files here from any app
  • Split a PDF three ways: every page as its own file, chosen pages as a new PDF, or ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10
  • Files go to Downloads / Merge PDF with clear names; share, open or print them
  • Next in one tap: make the file smaller, add a stamp or signature, pull out the text
  • No limits on files or pages, no watermark, no account
  • Only the pages you need: take 1-3, 5 from each file when merging
  • Parts of the right size: split into files up to 2, 5, 10 or 25 MB for upload portals and email; a merged PDF too heavy for email? «Split into parts» is right on the Done screen
  • Page tools: rotate, remove and reorder pages in one PDF
  • Passwords: open password-protected PDFs and lock your own
  • Page numbers and a watermark across every page
  • More: split every N pages, send all parts as one ZIP

Free and Pro

Every feature is free: a short ad plays for each result. Pro removes all ads — monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase forever. Restore a purchase: open Pro and tap “Restore”. Cancel a subscription: Google Play → Subscriptions.

Optional: leave a tip of any size from the menu. It unlocks nothing and helps make the next apps.

Everything happens on the phone

Files are merged and split on the phone itself; your documents are never uploaded. The internet is used only for ads in the free version and for Google Play purchases. Files leave the phone only when you share them.
